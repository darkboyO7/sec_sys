# PROMPT 46 – Authorized Attack-Surface Inventory and Exposure Governance

Repository: `D:\alt`

## Objective
Improve visibility into the externally observable attack surface of explicitly authorized assets while maintaining strict assessment boundaries.

### Inspect
Review existing asset discovery, DNS and TLS observations, service identification, scope authorization, exclusions, evidence provenance, monitoring, and asset identity resolution.

### Implement
1. Create or extend an exposure inventory that records authorized assets, observed services, relevant hostnames, certificates, exposure classifications, timestamps, and evidence references where supported.
2. Distinguish verified observations, imported observations, simulated results, and inferred relationships.
3. Track observation freshness and historical changes.
4. Detect meaningful changes such as a newly observed service, changed certificate, changed hostname association, or disappearance of a previously observed endpoint.
5. Distinguish disappearance of an observation from proof that a service was removed.
6. Revalidate authorization and exclusions immediately before every active assessment operation.
7. Prevent discoveries from automatically expanding the authorized scope.
8. Add explicit approval workflows for proposing newly discovered assets for inclusion.
9. Integrate changes with existing risk scoring, alerts, reporting, and scheduling.
10. Provide an exposure timeline and an asset-level evidence view.
11. Support deduplication without erasing historical identity or provenance.
12. Add APIs and a frontend view using existing conventions.

### Safety
Use existing approved passive adapters and controlled fixtures. Do not introduce unrestricted port scanning, raw-socket sweeps, brute forcing, exploit execution, or disruptive probes. Do not contact arbitrary discovered destinations.

### Tests
Test excluded targets, scope revocation, DNS changes, reused IP addresses, duplicate observations, stale evidence, simulated-versus-live labeling, and unauthorized asset inclusion.

### Acceptance
Every exposure record must identify its source, freshness, authorization context, and uncertainty. No observation may silently authorize further testing.

Run regression tests and builds. Report implemented adapters honestly and list unsupported data sources. Preserve unrelated work and the recovery mirror.
