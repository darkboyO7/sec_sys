## Requirements

3. Centralize authorization decisions using the repository's existing patterns instead of introducing competing RBAC implementations.

6. Ensure permissions are checked against the actual requested resource and current server-side state.
7. Preserve dual-approval or separation-of-duties controls where applicable.
