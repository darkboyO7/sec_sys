# PROMPT 52 — Fault Injection, Graceful Degradation and Recovery Verification

Repository: `D:\alt`

## Objective
Verify that the VAPT System remains safe and understandable when databases, workers, adapters, providers, and background tasks fail.

### Inspect
Review error handling, worker lifecycle, database transactions, event delivery, retries, health endpoints, evidence persistence, startup, and recovery logic.

### Implement
1. Define critical system capabilities and their failure dependencies.
2. Introduce deterministic fault-injection hooks for tests only.
3. Test database interruption, worker termination, adapter timeout, provider outage, disk-write failure, malformed responses, and interrupted background jobs.
4. Ensure critical evidence and audit records are not silently discarded when secondary services fail.
5. Prevent retries from creating duplicate assessments, notifications, or remediation actions.
6. Apply bounded timeouts, retry budgets, and backoff.
7. Separate readiness, liveness, dependency health, and degraded functionality.
8. Ensure health reporting does not expose secrets or internal sensitive data.
9. Recover incomplete jobs through existing persistent mechanisms.
10. Prevent automatic recovery from weakening scope restrictions, authorization, or approval requirements.
11. Add operator-readable failure classifications and recovery instructions.
12. Verify that the application reports partial success and incomplete work honestly.
13. Ensure fault injection cannot accidentally activate in a normal deployment.
14. Document which failures require manual intervention.

### Tests
Use controlled test fixtures to interrupt operations at important transaction boundaries. Verify persistence, idempotency, recovery, and truthful job status.

Do not deliberately corrupt the user's live database, delete real evidence, or interrupt unrelated processes.

### Acceptance
Critical failures must be detectable, recovery must be bounded and auditable, and incomplete operations must never be presented as successful completion.

Run relevant regression tests and builds. Report injected failure scenarios, recovery outcomes, remaining single points of failure, and exact test results.
